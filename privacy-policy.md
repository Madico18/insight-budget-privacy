# Privacy Policy for Insight Budget

**Last updated: September 5, 2026**

Insight Budget ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used when you use the Insight Budget mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

Insight Budget uses phone number authentication. When you create or access your account, your phone number is used for authentication and account management purposes.

Authentication is provided using Firebase services.

### Profile Information

If you provide profile information in the App, such as your name, username, or date of birth, this information may be stored in connection with your account.

### Financial Information

Insight Budget allows you to enter and manage personal budgeting information, including expenses, income, categories, budgets, and related records.

This information is provided by you and is used solely to provide the App's budgeting functionality.

Insight Budget does not connect to your bank accounts and does not process payments or financial transactions.

### Notifications

Insight Budget uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to provide push notifications related to the App.

A device identifier or notification token may be processed to deliver these notifications.

## How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through the App to:

- create and manage your account;
- authenticate you;
- provide budgeting and expense management features;
- store and synchronize your data;
- provide notifications related to the App;
- maintain and improve the functionality and security of the App.

## Data Storage and Service Providers

Insight Budget uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

These services may process information necessary to provide authentication, cloud data storage, synchronization, and push notifications.

For information about Google's privacy practices, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase where necessary to operate the App.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law.

## Advertising

The current version of Insight Budget does not display third-party advertising and does not use user information for advertising purposes.

If advertising is introduced in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your information may be retained while your account remains active or as necessary to provide the App's functionality.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at:

insight.budget@gmail.com

We will process valid deletion requests subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

## Children's Privacy

Insight Budget is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
